Ask us on Instagram
How do I book?
All bookings start with a DM on Instagram to @events.bytheocean — just send your date, occasion, guest count and location.
Where do you create your setups?
We’re based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, and serve Halifax and beyond — just share your location in your message.
How can I find out about pricing?
Every setup is custom, so for pricing, DM us your plans — guest count, location and any styling inspiration.
Do you style more than picnics?
Yes — we also style birthdays, bachelorettes, proposals, date nights and wellness events.
Are you booking 2026 events?
We are! Send your preferred date and a little about your celebration on Instagram to ask about availability.
